Read the problem statement and answer in short bullets:

- Who is the user, and what are they trying to finish in one sitting?
- What is the painful step today?
- What can a phone do here that a website cannot (camera, location, voice, notifications, offline)?
- What is the smallest demo a judge can complete in under two minutes?
- What will we explicitly not build?

Do not propose a schema or a screen list until those five answers are written.
